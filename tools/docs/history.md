# Greeblis Town: change history

Every change to the game went through a PR in this repo,
[GorboGreeblis/GreeblisTown](https://github.com/GorboGreeblis/GreeblisTown) (named JurassicGreeblisPark at first).
The game began as **Jurassic Greeblis Park**, a prehistoric clearing with dinosaurs; with #20 it became a small
Midwest town, with #31 it was renamed **Greeblis Town USA**, and with #98 simply **Greeblis Town** (the owner). Newest first.

## Owner decisions recorded along the way

The standing rules that came out of these are in [greeblis-rules.md](greeblis-rules.md); these are the other
decisions, with the PR that carried them.

- **The game:** it is called "Greeblis Town" (#98; "Greeblis Town USA" from #31). One repo from #51 on; the readable parcels' labels in the
  mail pile are the one exception to the privacy rule. A second, readable copy of the game lives at
  `tools/index.html` and is never compressed; the root `index.html` is the one that gets packed, and must still
  work as a Bitcoin ordinal (#77, a rule for every future game too). Three more rules for every game (#78): the
  final game must be fully playable as a Bitcoin ordinal, on ordinals.com and the other ordinal sites in
  particular; the final file must be as small as it can be without losing any content or quality; and the final
  game must be fully playable in the Meta Quest Browser, and every game is optimised for it (#82). Performance is always optimised, never at the expense of quality (#83). In these VR games nothing may ever flicker or z-fight (#85). In compression, file size always matters more than load
  time (#79). Greeblis Town is the bible: its lore, designs and style rule every future Greeblis game, its lore
  points never change, and the past games get updated to its world when they are worked on again (#79).
- **Creatures:**
  - Every model is final (#43); Montgummery's screen mouth is a flat line, with a pixel smile only for a while
    after a Blossom (#40).
  - Creatures first stood still in rows; from #47 some roam (Hennrick round the Portal, Gemtoe and the Gorbos in
    the park, the Dancers on the dance floor), and the rest keep a post.
  - Mama Gorbo is a bright, vibrant peach, with colourful goo dripping out of her (animated like goo, never
    clipping, no see-through ends), and shoots goo at the player when given a Blossom (#38).
  - Vignettes play with a 15% chance.
  - Sparkakus has the usual Talk and Blossom menu: a Blossom can be given before his quest is talked about or
    done; at the Event no creature offers a Blossom option (#67).
  - Birds in the park's trees, one to a tree, built from the owner's photos: a cardinal (#69) and a robin (#94);
    each has small animations and never flies or leaves its tree. Creature visits start every 22 to 32 s, twice as often as before (#70).
  - The Ancient is built from the owner's photos (bigger when that fits more of the photo), its stalks rooted in
    its head, with slight resting moves and no dance or planting (#72, #75).
  - The avatar's default outfit is a yellow sun dress, and her Wanted poster follows her design (#73).
- **Quests:** the Ancient Artifacts come from Dance battles, one from each Dancer's first win (#42, #44). For a
  while the Summoning Stone was owned from the start and all three quests started complete, only for testing
  (`TEST_QUESTS`, #43); that head start was removed, so a game starts with no quest or item progress.
- **The Garden Event:** it starts only from the Summoning Stone, whose runes stay dark until the player uses it
  holding the Stone (#36, #38). Without Seeds it is bare; with them the plants are the ones the creatures plant
  (#42), the first two Events each grow a batch and later ones grow nothing (#47), and plants persist for the
  session.
- **The church:** a garden window on each side wall, the right one as in Greeblis City and the left a copy with the
  figure and Mama Gorbo swapped (#37); figures in the windows have no faces; the windows are final (#42). Nothing
  is placed inside the church, and the park has no flowerbeds (#51). The outside is built after the owner's
  photos of a real church, with the stained glass and the inside kept exactly as they were (#71); the inside is
  final, and its secret room takes its details only from the owner's photo (#75).
- **The town:**
  - Every path leads to a kiosk or a building and ends there (#36).
  - The Library is open: only rows of bookcases spaced out, the LIBRARY banner behind them, no walls or raised
    platforms (#38, #40).
  - The dance floor is one 10 × 32-tile rectangle from the bar's north end, twice the bar's length (#39, #41); King
    Dino and his kit stand on the grass behind its south half (#40).
  - No cars (#36), no far scenery (#47), no benches or trash cans, and no floating pebbles round the Summoning
    Stone (#51).
  - Every door is wood, with a transom over storefront doors; Mistergummery's uses Montgummery's teal instead of
    yellow, has no roof burger, and its pylon is just the M (#51).
  - The player started just south of the Summoning Stone, facing the church (#51); from #87 they start at the
    edge of the park, just inside the west fence north of the Juice Bar, facing King Dino's billboard across the
    road, so it is the first thing seen (above all in VR) and the park is a surprise behind them when they turn
    round. The billboard's banner is wide and three lines tall so it reads from there, and the street lamp that
    stood in front of the board from the spawn is gone (#87).
  - The billboard stands outside the park but close to it, on the corner lot across the road from the park's
    north-west corner (#66). Nothing outside the park takes anything from the Ancient (#68).
  - The readable parcels round the mail heap are spaced out, never stacked or made obvious, their labels always
    facing out (#68).
